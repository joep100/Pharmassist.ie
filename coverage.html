Coverage

Twelve kilometres of O'Connell Bridge

That's the whole city and most of the county: Blanchardstown to Blackrock, Finglas to Tallaght, Palmerstown to Portmarnock. Check any address below.

Fully covered

Eighteen areas we always reach

These sit completely inside the 12km circle, so every address in them is a yes. No checking required.

On the boundary

Eight areas we check case by case

The circle cuts through the middle of these. Blanchardstown village is inside it, Clonee isn't. Dún Laoghaire is inside, Killiney isn't. So we look up the individual address rather than guess from the postcode.

Outside these areas we're not running deliveries yet. We open a new area once there's enough there to fill a run, so if your pharmacy sits further out, tell us. Expansion follows the pharmacies, not the other way round.

One thing worth saying

Twelve kilometres, measured straight

Our range is a circle drawn on a map, which is a clean rule but not the same as a drive. Tallaght is ten kilometres from O'Connell Bridge as the crow flies and a considerably longer run at half five on a Friday. A stretch of the circle to the east is Dublin Bay.

In practice that means the edges are a conversation rather than a hard line. If an address falls just outside and the timing works, ring us. We would rather make the delivery than win an argument about geometry.
