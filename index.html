Their next day is really the day after

A national carrier's "next day" means the next day after dispatch, and dispatch is tomorrow. Ours means the next day after ordering. Order before your cutoff and it goes today. After it, tomorrow. Never three to five working days.

Stock that's already here

If what you sell is in Dublin, you are closer to your customer than any warehouse in Britain will ever be. Most shops never use that.

Awkward things, handled

Fragile, chilled, valuable, oddly shaped. A parcel network is built for identical boxes. We aren't.

Nothing to switch off

Keep your carrier for everywhere else. This is a Dublin lane alongside it, not a replacement.

Three things we don't do

Worth saying plainly, because they are the questions everyone asks.

We don't take your customer

No marketplace, no app for them to join, no competing shop. They arrived as yours and they stay yours.

We don't open the bag

You pack it and seal it. We carry it closed and prove we delivered it.

We don't tie you in

No contract, no minimum, no notice. Stop whenever it suits you.
