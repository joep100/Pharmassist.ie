How a delivery runs

Three steps, and two of them are ours

Nothing about your dispensing changes. The only new thing is a bag going into a different pair of hands.

01

You dispense and seal

Exactly as you do today. Your pharmacist checks the bag, seals it and labels it with the patient's name and Eircode. That's your part finished.

02

We collect

A courier calls to your counter and scans each bag out of your shop. From that moment it's on our record, not in a grey area between the two of you.

03

The patient has it today

Delivered to the door, handed to a named person, with proof of delivery back to you the same day. Nobody waits until tomorrow.

Why pharmacies use us

Same day is the gap nobody big has closed

The large groups have the app, the online doctor and a branch near everyone. What none of them has is a way to put a prescription in a patient's hand today. Closing it means somebody driving a bag to a door, every day, in every town they trade in. You only need that in one shop. They would need it in a hundred.

Fridge lines, not just boxes

Cold chain is where most couriers stop. We carry it every day, GLP-1 included. You pack the cold box as usual, and same-day transit means it never has to hold overnight.

Clear the collection shelf

Bags sitting waiting are money tied up and stock you can't shift. Delivery turns a shelf of uncollected prescriptions into completed sales.

Serve the ones who can't come in

Housebound patients, a parent with a sick child, anyone mid-shift. Today that's a staff member in their own car for an hour. It doesn't need to be.

Where the line sits

Three things we don't do

Most delivery services want to become the pharmacy. We're the opposite, and we'd rather be blunt about it up front.

We don't dispense

You dispense, you check, you seal. We carry a closed bag and we don't open it. Responsibility for what's inside stays with your pharmacist, where it belongs.

We don't advise

No clinical questions, no substitutions, no counselling at the door. If a patient asks a driver something clinical, the answer is your phone number.

We don't take your patient

No competing pharmacy, no marketplace, no app quietly moving them somewhere cheaper. They arrived as your patient and they stay your patient.

Independently certified

Audited, not just promised

Handing patient medicines to somebody else is a real decision, and it shouldn't have to rest on our word. Our operation is certified by the NSAI to ISO 9001 for quality management and ISO 14001 for environmental management.

In practice that means the way we handle, track and hand over your bags is written down, followed, and checked by an auditor from outside the company. It's also the paperwork your own procedures will want to see.
