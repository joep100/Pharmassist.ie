For patients

We bring it. Your pharmacy dispenses it.

Pharmassist is the delivery part. Your own pharmacy prepares your prescription, seals it, and hands it to one of our couriers. We bring it to your door the same day.

You can't order from us directly, so ask your pharmacy whether they use us. Plenty across Dublin do.

On the day

What happens after your pharmacy books it

01

It's sealed before we see it

Your pharmacist prepares and seals your bag with your name on it. We never open it and we're never told what's inside.

02

A courier brings it to you

The same day, to the address your pharmacy gave us. We'll ring your mobile if we can't find the door.

03

Someone has to take it

We hand it over in person and note who accepted it. If nobody's in, the bag goes back to your pharmacy, which is safer than a doorstep.

Common questions

Things worth knowing

Who do I ring?

Anything about your medicine, whether that's a dose, a side effect or a substitution, ring your pharmacy. They know your prescription and we don't. Anything about where your bag is, ring us on 01 000 0000.

Will the driver know what I'm taking?

No. The bag is sealed at the pharmacy counter before we collect it, and we hold only your name, address and phone number. Our couriers can't tell one bag from another.

Can somebody else take it in?

Usually yes, as long as they're an adult at the address and your pharmacy hasn't asked us to hand it to you personally. We record who accepted it either way.

What does it cost me?

That's between you and your pharmacy. They arrange delivery and set what, if anything, they charge for it. We bill the pharmacy, not you.
